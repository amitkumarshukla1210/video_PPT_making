Slides

Add screenshots with notes, or import a PowerPoint. On any slide you can record yourself explaining it — your video appears in the corner and your own voice is used.

Tip: name files 01_cost-summary.png, 02_bom.png… — they are added in that order and the name becomes the slide title. One line of notes = one bullet.

Voice clone — speak in your own voice

Record or upload 10–30 seconds of yourself reading clearly in a quiet room. The app then speaks any slide's narration in your voice, in 23 languages including Hindi. Everything stays on this laptop.

Read this aloud: “Design to Cost helps us control product cost from the very first design. In this video I will show how commodity prices, exchange rates and part costs come together, so every decision is made with the full cost in view.”

Step 4

Presenter in the corner

Slides with your recording (Step 2) always show your video. For the other slides you can show your photo.

A photo does not move its lips — for real lip movement, record yourself on the slide (phone or webcam). See README › Presenter for the optional AI talking-photo add-on.
