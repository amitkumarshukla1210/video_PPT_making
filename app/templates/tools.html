Create pictures from a prompt

⭐ Get the best pictures (closest to ChatGPT) FLUX.2 klein draws real faces, hands, clothes and light, and follows long, detailed prompts (up to about 300 words). One-time download of about 4.6 GB; after that it works offline. Needs 16 GB of memory; about 1 minute a picture. Close other big programs while it draws.
🎬 Get film-quality pictures (faster) Stable Diffusion XL Lightning draws sharp, cinematic 1024-pixel pictures. One-time download of about 4.8 GB; after that it works offline, about 10–30 seconds a picture.

Tip: Best quality reads long prompts (about 300 words); Film and Fast read only the first 60–70 words. Put a character's look in 1 Character once, then write short scenes like “@Ram and @Sita in a forest hut at sunset”.
